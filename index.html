MeetReady

A native macOS menu bar app that keeps you on top of your meetings

Never miss a meeting again with smart alerts, one-click joining, and live participant tracking for Google Meet.

Download on the Mac App Store

Features

🔔 Smart Alerts

Get timely notifications before your meetings start

🚀 One-Click Join

Join Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, or Webex calls instantly from the menu bar

👥 Participant Tracking

See who's in your meetings in real-time

📅 Calendar Integration

Seamlessly syncs with your Google Calendar

🌐 Multi-Platform Support

Works with Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Webex

👤 Multi-Account Support

Connect up to 3 Google accounts with per-account calendars and Chrome profiles

⌨️ Instant Access Anywhere

Join your next meeting from any app with a global keyboard shortcut

About MeetReady

MeetReady is a lightweight, native macOS application designed to help you stay organized and never miss important meetings. It lives in your menu bar, providing quick access to your upcoming Google Meet sessions.
